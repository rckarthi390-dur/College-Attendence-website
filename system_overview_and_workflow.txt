1. SYSTEM EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The College Attendance Management System is a role-based web application designed
to handle daily attendance marking, historical modifications with automated audit 
logging, class analytics, student leave/OD applications, and institutional administration.

Key System Highlights:
• 3 Role-Based Dashboards: Faculty/Staff, Student, and Administrator.
• Standalone Single-File & Full Modular React Implementations.
• Local Storage Persistence (no cloud or complex DB required for local demo).
• Express.js + LowDB API Backend included for full-stack deployment.
• 100% Mobile Responsive UI built with Tailwind CSS & Google Fonts (Inter).

2. USER ROLES & DETAILED WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ROLE 1: FACULTY / STAFF DASHBOARD]
Workflow 1.1: Daily Manual Attendance Marking
  - Step 1: Select Department, Course/Subject, Section (e.g. Sec A), and Date.
  - Step 2: System automatically loads the student roster for that class.
  - Step 3: Faculty can use quick bulk shortcuts: "Mark All Present" or "Mark All Absent".
  - Step 4: Toggle individual student statuses:
            • Present (P)
            • Absent (A)
            • Late (L)
            • On-Duty (OD)
  - Step 5: Search for specific students using the real-time search bar.
  - Step 6: Click "Save Attendance Session" to persist records.

Workflow 1.2: Unlimited Historical Attendance Modifications
  - Step 1: Open the Date Picker and select ANY past date or session.
  - Step 2: Staff can edit attendance entries without session locks, time limits, 
            or administrator approval requirements.
  - Step 3: On saving, the system automatically captures modification history.

Workflow 1.3: Audit Log Generation
  - Step 1: Automatically records an audit entry whenever past records are changed:
            • Faculty ID / Name
            • Timestamp (Date & Time)
            • Old Status → New Status (e.g. Absent → On-Duty)
            • Reason for modification
  - Step 2: View personal audit log history directly in the "My Audit Log" tab.

Workflow 1.4: Class Analytics & Low Attendance Alerts
  - Step 1: Visual summaries powered by Recharts (Bar chart & Pie chart).
  - Step 2: Automatic flagging of students with attendance below 75% threshold.
  - Step 3: Export attendance data to CSV file with one click.

Workflow 1.5: Student Leave & OD Request Review
  - Step 1: View incoming Absence Leave and On-Duty (OD) applications from students.
  - Step 2: Click "Approve" or "Reject" to update request status.


[ROLE 2: STUDENT DASHBOARD]
Workflow 2.1: Overview & Subject Breakdowns
  - Step 1: Circular Percentage Gauge displaying overall attendance percentage.
  - Step 2: Subject-wise breakdown cards with color-coded status badges:
            • Green Badge: ≥ 75% (Passing / Meeting threshold)
            • Red Badge:   < 75% (Low attendance alert)
  - Step 3: Breakdown metrics per subject: Present count, Absent count, Late count, OD count.

Workflow 2.2: Attendance Log & Update Badges
  - Step 1: Detailed date-wise attendance status log per subject.
  - Step 2: Clear visual indicators: entries modified by staff display a highlighted 
            "UPDATED BY FACULTY" badge to show that a past entry was revised.

Workflow 2.3: Leave & On-Duty (OD) Application
  - Step 1: Form to select Request Type: "Absence Leave" or "On-Duty (OD)".
  - Step 2: Fill From Date, To Date, and Reason / Justification.
  - Step 3: Submit application to send it to Faculty & Admin for approval.
  - Step 4: Track status (Pending, Approved, Rejected) or cancel pending requests.


[ROLE 3: ADMIN DASHBOARD]
Workflow 3.1: User Management (CRUD)
  - Step 1: View all system users (Students, Faculty, Administrators).
  - Step 2: Search by Name, Email, Roll Number, or Employee ID.
  - Step 3: Filter by Role or Department.
  - Step 4: Add new users or edit existing user details (Roll No, Section, Year, Dept).
  - Step 5: Remove users permanently.

Workflow 3.2: Course & Department Management
  - Step 1: Create, edit, and delete Courses and Departments.
  - Step 2: Assign HODs (Heads of Department) and Faculty instructors to courses.

Workflow 3.3: Master Audit Log
  - Step 1: System-wide audit trail recording all staff attendance modifications over time.
  - Step 2: Filter by Faculty Member, Course, or Date range.
  - Step 3: Click "Export Audit Trail (CSV)" to download the complete audit history.

Workflow 3.4: System Settings & Official Reports
  - Step 1: Configure Mandatory Attendance Threshold (e.g. 75% rule).
  - Step 2: Update Institution Name and Academic Year (e.g. 2024-25).
  - Step 3: Click "Download Master PDF Report" to generate an official institutional report 
            containing table formatting, color badges, and threshold compliance status.
